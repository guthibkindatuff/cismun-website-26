About the Committee

The Economic and Social Council is the United Nations' central platform for reflection, debate, and innovative thinking on sustainable development. It coordinates the economic, social, and related work of 15 UN specialized agencies, functional commissions and five regional commissions.

At CISMUN 2026, ECOSOC will focus on integrating economic, social, and environmental policies to achieve sustainable development goals.

Agenda

  1. Promoting decent work and economic growth in developing nations
  2. Addressing inequality through progressive taxation and social protection
  3. Strengthening global cooperation on digital infrastructure
  4. Enhancing access to quality education for marginalized communities
  5. Building resilient health systems post-pandemic
