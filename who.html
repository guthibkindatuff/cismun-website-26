About the Committee

The World Health Organization is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for international public health. WHO's primary role is to direct and coordinate international health within the United Nations system.

At CISMUN 2026, WHO will address global health emergencies, universal health coverage, and health equity.

Agenda

  1. Strengthening pandemic preparedness and response systems
  2. Addressing the growing burden of non-communicable diseases
  3. Promoting mental health and well-being in the workplace
  4. Ensuring equitable access to vaccines and essential medicines
  5. Tackling antimicrobial resistance through global cooperation
