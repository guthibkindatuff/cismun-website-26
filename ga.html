About the Committee

The General Assembly is the main deliberative, policymaking and representative organ of the United Nations. Comprising all 193 Member States, it provides a forum for multilateral discussion of the full spectrum of international issues covered by the Charter of the United Nations.

At CISMUN 2026, the General Assembly will simulate its plenary sessions, focusing on pressing global challenges such as sustainable development, international peace and security, and human rights.

Agenda

  1. Strengthening UN coordination in humanitarian assistance in complex emergencies
  2. Promoting sustainable tourism for economic growth and environmental protection
  3. Addressing the impact of climate change on global food security
  4. Enhancing international cooperation in combating illicit financial flows
  5. Protecting the rights of indigenous peoples in the context of climate change adaptation
