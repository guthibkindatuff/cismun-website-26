About the Committee

The Human Rights Council is an inter-governmental body within the United Nations system responsible for strengthening the promotion and protection of human rights around the globe. It addresses situations of human rights violations and makes recommendations on them.

At CISMUN 2026, the Human Rights Council will examine pressing human rights issues and work towards strengthening international human rights law.

Agenda

  1. Protecting the rights of refugees and asylum seekers
  2. Addressing discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity
  3. Ensuring access to clean water and sanitation as a human right
  4. Combating hate speech and extremism online
  5. Promoting the rights of persons with disabilities in humanitarian crises
