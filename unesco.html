About the Committee

UNESCO is the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. It seeks to build peace through international cooperation in Education, the Sciences and Culture. UNESCO's programmes contribute to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals defined in Agenda 2030.

At CISMUN 2026, UNESCO will focus on preserving cultural heritage, promoting education for all, and advancing scientific cooperation.

Agenda

  1. Protecting world heritage sites in conflict zones
  2. Promoting STEM education for girls in developing countries
  3. Addressing the impact of artificial intelligence on cultural diversity
  4. Strengthening international cooperation in open science
  5. Preserving indigenous languages and traditional knowledge
